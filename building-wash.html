XTERA Building Washing
What it is

Remove the grime, mould and algae, not the paint.

Auckland's damp, shaded and coastal conditions let mould, algae, lichen, salt, dirt and cobwebs build up on walls and cladding. A building wash lifts all of it off, brightens the exterior, and helps protect the surface for longer.

We clean homes, apartments, offices, retail and industrial sites, and body corporate properties, including multi-storey buildings, across Auckland.
