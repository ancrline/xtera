What it is

Bring tired surfaces back to life.

Driveways and paths collect dirt, oil, moss and algae. They look tired, and wet moss and algae make them slippery. A proper clean restores the surface and makes the whole property look cared for.

We clean homes, commercial car parks and forecourts, and strata common areas across Auckland.

Signs it's time

Is it time for a surface clean?

If you notice any of these, your driveway or paths are probably overdue.

Slippery paths, steps or driveway when wet
Green moss or algae growing on surfaces
Oil, tyre or rust stains
Dark, dull or patchy concrete and paving
Weeds pushing through joints and cracks
Cars parked on stained, grimy surfaces
